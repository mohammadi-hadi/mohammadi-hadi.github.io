01About

Hello! I’m Hadi

At AcademicTransfer I build the LLM matching and ranking behind the recruitment platform of 22 Dutch research universities and university medical centres. It reads 10,000+ applications a month. So far it has cut time-to-hire by 20% and improved candidate–job matching by 17%.

My PhD at Utrecht University, defended in September 2026, asked why a model gives the answer it does and whether that explanation can be trusted. Its papers appeared at *SEM 2026, in the CLIN Journal and Applied Sciences, and at ACL 2025 and ECAI 2025 workshops. This autumn adds a long paper at INLG 2026 and my first solo paper at a NeurIPS 2026 workshop.

03Research

What I work on

Explainable NLP

NLP models whose decisions domain experts can inspect and question, through post-hoc methods and token-level explanations.

Cultural fairness in LLMs

How LLMs represent cultural variation in moral judgments and social norms, and how to evaluate their alignment across cultures.

LLM evaluation

Interpretable chain-of-thought and LLM-as-judge evaluation, to tell when a model’s predictions and explanations can be trusted.

Human-AI annotation reliability

Whether LLM annotations can stand in for human ones: model outputs against human rationalizations, and demographic bias in annotation.

PhD thesis: Let Me Explain! Explainable NLP for Understanding Large Language Models
